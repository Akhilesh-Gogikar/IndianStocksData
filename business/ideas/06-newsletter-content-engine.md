# Newsletter Content Engine

## Thesis

A content automation system that turns Indian equities data into sector recaps, earnings summaries, and market dashboards.

## Customer

Finance creators, newsletters, media teams, communities, and internal research desks.

## Product

- Sector trend summaries.
- Earnings update drafts.
- Top movers and event calendars.
- Chart packs and publish-ready snippets.

## Data Inputs

Quotes, sectors, financial deltas, events, corporate actions, and company metadata.

## Monetization

Creator subscription, managed content service, and white-label publisher tooling.

## MVP

Weekly sector recap generator with charts and no buy/sell language.

## Risks

Copyright, data licensing, and accidental stock recommendation language.

## Next Steps

Create a "market week in review" template and generate it from local snapshots.
