# Data Quality And Anomaly Feed

## Thesis

A monitoring product that detects stale data, missing fields, outliers, unusual financial movements, and corporate-action inconsistencies.

## Customer

Research teams, fintechs, funds, data vendors, and internal data engineering teams.

## Product

- Freshness monitoring.
- Missing-field and schema-drift alerts.
- Price and financial outlier detection.
- Corporate-action consistency checks.

## Data Inputs

Historical snapshots, current parsed records, source timestamps, price series, and event records.

## Monetization

API subscription, alert feed, and enterprise monitoring contracts.

## MVP

Daily anomaly report for 500 stocks with freshness, missing fields, and outlier checks.

## Risks

False positives, missing ground truth, and limited willingness to pay unless positioned as a reliability layer.

## Next Steps

Add snapshot versioning and compute day-over-day field diffs.
