# Portfolio X-Ray Tool

## Thesis

A holdings analysis tool that shows concentration, sector exposure, valuation profile, volatility, and fundamental quality across a user's portfolio.

## Customer

Retail investors, family offices, and registered advisers who want portfolio diagnostics.

## Product

- CSV holdings upload.
- Exposure by sector, market cap, factor, and stock.
- Portfolio-level valuation and quality metrics.
- Risk flags for concentration, volatility, and event exposure.

## Data Inputs

Holdings, current prices, sectors, ratios, beta or volatility estimates, and market-cap data.

## Monetization

Freemium scan, paid reports, adviser team accounts, and white-label versions.

## MVP

CSV upload with sector concentration, top holdings, valuation summary, and volatility snapshot.

## Risks

Personalized advice boundary. Keep output diagnostic unless operating under proper registration.

## Next Steps

Define upload format, map tickers to company master, and generate one static HTML report.
