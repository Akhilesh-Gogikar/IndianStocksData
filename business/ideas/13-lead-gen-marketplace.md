# Lead-Gen Marketplace

## Thesis

A marketplace that connects investors to SEBI-registered advisers, research analysts, brokers, or wealth professionals.

## Customer

Investors looking for help and registered professionals looking for qualified leads.

## Product

- Verified professional profiles.
- Matching flow by need, asset class, fee model, language, and location.
- Educational pre-qualification and inquiry routing.

## Data Inputs

Professional registry data, user preferences, inquiry metadata, and compliance disclosures.

## Monetization

Professional subscription, verified profile fees, referral fee where allowed, and sponsored placement with disclosure.

## MVP

Directory of verified professionals plus lead capture form and manual matching.

## Risks

High compliance sensitivity. Must verify registrations, avoid unregistered advice, and handle referral fee rules carefully.

## Next Steps

Define verification workflow and legal boundaries before building the matching product.
