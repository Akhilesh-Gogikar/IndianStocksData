# Watchlist Alert App

## Thesis

A personal monitoring app that alerts users when companies they follow have price, fundamental, event, or announcement changes.

## Customer

Retail investors and analysts who already know which companies they care about.

## Product

- Watchlists by user.
- Alerts for price movement, valuation threshold, result updates, corporate actions, announcements, and score changes.
- Daily digest email or push notification.

## Data Inputs

Quotes, fundamentals, corporate actions, announcements, historical snapshots, and user-defined alert rules.

## Monetization

Freemium watchlists, paid alert frequency, premium digest, and multi-watchlist plans.

## MVP

Manual ticker watchlist plus daily email digest for price and fundamental changes.

## Risks

If alerts imply "act now" or "buy/sell", the product becomes compliance-sensitive.

## Next Steps

Create a watchlist schema, snapshot diff job, and one daily digest template.
