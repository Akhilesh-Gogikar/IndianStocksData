# AI Research Workspace

## Thesis

An analyst workspace that summarizes company pages, explains financial changes, compares peers, and drafts risk notes from structured data.

## Customer

Analysts, retail investors, finance creators, small funds, and registered research teams.

## Product

- Company research notebooks.
- AI summaries grounded in source fields.
- Peer delta analysis.
- Earnings and announcement summaries.
- Citation-backed report drafts.

## Data Inputs

Structured financials, quotes, events, peer tables, announcements, and source metadata.

## Monetization

Seat-based SaaS, premium AI credits, and B2B team workspaces.

## MVP

One company page with generated summary, peer comparison, change log, and cited data table.

## Risks

Hallucination, data rights, and compliance drift into investment recommendation.

## Next Steps

Build a retrieval layer over normalized company records and force every generated claim to cite a source field.
