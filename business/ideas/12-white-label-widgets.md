# White-Label Widgets

## Thesis

Embeddable Indian stock cards, peer tables, financial charts, and sector widgets for publishers and communities.

## Customer

Finance blogs, investor communities, education sites, newsletters, and fintech apps.

## Product

- Stock profile card.
- Peer comparison table.
- Financial chart embed.
- Sector snapshot widget.
- Configurable branding.

## Data Inputs

Company profile, quotes, ratios, financials, peer mappings, and source freshness.

## Monetization

Monthly license, pageview-based pricing, and enterprise embeds.

## MVP

One embeddable stock card and one peer table with script-tag integration.

## Risks

Data redistribution and licensing are central. Widgets expose data directly to third parties.

## Next Steps

Prototype widgets over internal data, then solve licensing before commercial deployment.
