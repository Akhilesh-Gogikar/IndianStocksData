# B2B Dashboard For RIAs, RAs, And PMS Firms

## Thesis

A workflow dashboard for registered finance professionals who need faster research, monitoring, and client-ready analytics.

## Customer

SEBI-registered Investment Advisers, Research Analysts, PMS teams, and wealth desks.

## Product

- Research workflow dashboard.
- Watchlist and client-list monitoring.
- Exportable charts and data tables.
- Report drafting tools with source trails.

## Data Inputs

Company fundamentals, prices, events, client watchlists, model watchlists, and report templates.

## Monetization

Seat-based B2B SaaS, team plans, onboarding fee, and premium data add-ons.

## MVP

Dashboard for a registered professional to track 50 companies and export report-ready tables.

## Risks

Still needs data licensing, but advice compliance is cleaner because the customer is the regulated professional.

## Next Steps

Interview 5 RIA or RA users and identify their repetitive research workflows.
