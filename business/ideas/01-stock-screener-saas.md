# Stock Screener SaaS

## Thesis

A filterable Indian equities dashboard for valuation, growth, quality, events, peer comparison, and scorecard-style discovery.

## Customer

Retail investors, independent analysts, finance creators, and small advisory teams that need faster idea discovery.

## Product

- Screener builder with filters for valuation, growth, profitability, leverage, liquidity, events, and peer metrics.
- Saved screens, watchlists, exports, and scheduled non-advisory alerts.
- Company detail pages with normalized financials and peer context.

## Data Inputs

Company master, fundamentals, ratios, quote snapshots, events, announcements, peer groups, sector tags, and freshness metadata.

## Monetization

Freemium for basic screens, paid plans for saved screens, exports, alerts, advanced factors, and API access.

## MVP

Build 20 high-confidence filters across 500 liquid stocks, with saved screens and CSV export.

## Risks

Data licensing, stale data, and accidentally drifting into recommendation language.

## Next Steps

Define the first 20 fields, normalize them into one table, and build a local Streamlit or FastAPI plus React prototype.
