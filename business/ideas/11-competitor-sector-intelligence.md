# Competitor And Sector Intelligence

## Thesis

A B2B dashboard that helps companies, consultants, and founders track listed peers and sector-level signals.

## Customer

Founders, strategy teams, consultants, corporate development teams, and analysts outside capital markets.

## Product

- Sector dashboards.
- Listed peer benchmarking.
- Margin, growth, valuation, and capital allocation comparisons.
- Event and announcement monitoring.

## Data Inputs

Sector tags, peer groups, financial statements, ratios, events, and company metadata.

## Monetization

B2B subscription, consulting reports, and custom dashboards.

## MVP

Pick 3 sectors and build peer dashboards with growth, margins, valuation, and announcement timelines.

## Risks

Sector classification quality and customer acquisition outside finance communities.

## Next Steps

Choose a high-value vertical like SaaS services, specialty chemicals, banks, or consumer brands.
