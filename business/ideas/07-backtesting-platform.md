# Backtesting Platform

## Thesis

A platform where users test Indian equities factor strategies using fundamentals, prices, and event filters.

## Customer

Retail quants, students, analysts, creators, and small research teams.

## Product

- Strategy builder using filters and rebalance rules.
- Historical returns, drawdowns, turnover, and benchmark comparison.
- Exportable methodology reports.

## Data Inputs

Point-in-time prices, financials, corporate actions, survivorship metadata, and benchmark data.

## Monetization

Subscription, educational licenses, and premium datasets.

## MVP

Monthly rebalance backtest for top N stocks by user-defined filters over a clean historical subset.

## Risks

Survivorship bias, lookahead bias, corporate action adjustment errors, and users treating backtests as recommendations.

## Next Steps

Build a bias-aware backtest contract before adding UI.
