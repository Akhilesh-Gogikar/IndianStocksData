# Education Product

## Thesis

A learning product that teaches stock analysis using real Indian company data, exercises, and simulations.

## Customer

Students, beginner investors, finance clubs, and retail learners.

## Product

- Lessons on ratios, financial statements, valuation, sectors, and risk.
- Interactive company case studies.
- Quizzes and simulated portfolios.
- Non-advisory learning dashboards.

## Data Inputs

Company profiles, financial statements, ratios, price history, sector mappings, and glossary metadata.

## Monetization

Course fee, subscription, campus licenses, and cohort workshops.

## MVP

Ten lessons using 20 well-known Indian companies and interactive ratio exercises.

## Risks

Must avoid promising investment outcomes or turning lessons into live stock tips.
