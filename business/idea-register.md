| 01 | Stock screener SaaS | Retail investors, analysts | Subscription | Medium | Filterable dashboard over fundamentals, valuation, events, and peers |
| 02 | Watchlist alert app | Retail investors | Freemium, subscription | Medium | Watchlist plus non-advisory alerts for price, events, and fundamentals |
| 03 | Indian equities API | Developers, fintechs, research teams | Usage-based API | Medium | Normalized company, quote, financial, event, and peer endpoints |
| 04 | AI research workspace | Analysts, investors, small funds | Subscription, seats | Medium | Company summaries, financial deltas, peer comparisons, risk notes |

| 10 | Education product | Students, retail learners | Course, subscription | Low | Learn stock analysis with real company data and simulations |

| 12 | White-label widgets | Publishers, communities, fintechs | License, usage fee | Medium | Embeddable stock cards, peer tables, and financial charts |
| 13 | Lead-gen marketplace | Investors, SEBI-registered professionals | Referral, subscription | High | Directory and matching flow for registered advisers or analysts |

## Prioritization Shortlist

1. Indian equities API: strongest foundation product because every other idea can reuse it.
2. Stock screener SaaS: fastest consumer-facing wedge if data rights are solved.
3. B2B RIA/RA/PMS dashboard: cleaner monetization because registered professionals carry advice responsibility.
4. Data quality/anomaly feed: narrow, defensible, and less dependent on making investment claims.
5. AI research workspace: high-value product, but needs careful compliance wording and source licensing.
