# IndianStocksData Business Ideas

This folder is the working home for business models that can be built around the Indian equities data pipeline.

The current ideas are split into two categories:

- Lower-regulatory tooling and data products: screeners, alerts, APIs, dashboards, education, widgets, anomaly feeds, and content workflows.
- Regulated or compliance-sensitive products: paid stock recommendations, personalized advice, model portfolios, and lead generation involving advisers or research analysts.

Before commercialization, validate:

1. Data rights: do not resell, redistribute, or commercially exploit scraped third-party data unless the source terms or a license allow it.
2. SEBI scope: avoid buy, sell, hold, target price, model portfolio, or personalized allocation language unless operating under the right registration.
3. Data quality: maintain source timestamps, freshness checks, missing-field handling, and audit trails.
4. User promise: sell workflow, analytics, education, or infrastructure unless the business is intentionally built as a regulated research or advisory entity.

## Files

- `idea-register.md`: one-page comparison of all business ideas.
- `data-and-compliance-notes.md`: shared constraints for every idea.
- `domain-server-launch-plan.md`: concrete domain, server, DNS, and launch execution plan.
- `ideas/`: individual briefs for each business model.
- `templates/idea-brief-template.md`: reusable format for future ideas.
